What is Flourishbot?

Flourishbot is a private AI agent that lives in your pocket (Telegram) and connects directly to your Microsoft email, calendar, and the Flourish CRM. It handles the admin work so you can focus on selling.

Built by: Michael (B2BEE) • Managed by: Michael • Controlled by: You

Week 1 — What It Will Do

  • Morning Brief — Every day at 08:00: meetings, unread emails, overdue tasks.
  • Email Triage — Reads your Microsoft inbox, flags urgent items, drafts replies in your voice.
  • CRM Sync — Updates lead stages, logs calls, reminds you to follow up.
  • Meeting Prep — Before every call: context + suggested talking points.
  • Quick Queries — "Who haven't I spoken to in 14 days?" / "What's my pipeline value?"

Month 1 — Full Autonomy

  • Send on Your Behalf — Standard emails sent without asking after training.
  • Calendar Management — Propose slots, send invites, reschedule conflicts.
  • Voice Messages — You send voice notes, it transcribes and acts.
  • Prospect Research — Before meetings, pull company info and suggest angles.
