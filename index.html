Compliance Stage Auto-Nudge (Prototype)

The highest-margin win: resolve debts before sending an enforcement agent. The Taking Control of Goods Regulations mandate a £75 Compliance Stage fee. Every case resolved at this stage saves £235 in Enforcement Stage costs.

Sample automation flow:

Day 0 — Debtor batch uploaded to portal
Day 1 — SMS: "Empira: A warrant has been issued. Pay or arrange now: [portal link]" Day 3 — Email: "Final reminder before agent dispatch. Set up a plan in 2 minutes." Day 5 — SMS: "Agent scheduled for [DATE]. Avoid fees — call 01925 984 020 or pay online." Day 7 — Agent dispatched (only for non-responders)

Result: 15–25% of debtors pay at Compliance Stage vs. 5% without nudging.

GDPR lawful basis: Legitimate Interest (private debt) / Performance of Public Task (council debt). No consent required. ECB and CIVEA encourage pre-enforcement engagement.
